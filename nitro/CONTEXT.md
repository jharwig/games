# Nitro Racing

Third-person 3D arcade time-trial racing game, cartoon (cel-shaded) look.
Designed by Alex. One lap against the clock; collect coins to unlock cars;
beat your own best time.

## Language

**Track**:
One of six closed loops, each a themed world (Speedway, Beach, Dirt Rally,
Ice Lake, Volcano, Space) with its own road surface, scenery, music and
ambience. All tracks are open from the start.

**Road**:
The drivable ribbon along a track's centreline. Off the road is slow ground,
except in Space where off the road is a fall.

**Checkpoint**:
One of six gates around a lap (the start line is the first). Falling off or
burning in lava puts the car back at the last checkpoint passed.
_Avoid_: respawn point, spawn

**Coin**:
A pickup on the road. Bronze = 1 (common), silver = 5, gold = 10 (rare).
Coins are banked the instant they are touched and never lost.

**Bank**:
The player's saved coin total, spent in the garage to unlock cars.

**Car**:
One of five: F1 Car, Frog, Ghost, Magnet, Tank. Each drives a little
differently and has exactly one Ability.

**Ability**:
The car's special move: Boost, Jump, Phase, Magnet, Smash. Three uses per
race, activated with Space or the on-screen ability button.
_Avoid_: power-up, item

**Pad**:
A marked patch on the road. A speed pad (green arrows) launches the car
forward; a slow pad (red arrows) drags it down.

**Obstacle**:
Something on the road that slows or spins the car when hit: cones, rocks,
tyre walls, oil slicks, dunes, ice cracks, lava pools, asteroids. Tank
smashes them, Ghost phases through them, Frog jumps over them.

**Best time**:
The fastest finish saved for a track, with which car set it.

**Ghost**:
The translucent replay of the best-time run that drives the track alongside
the player. Ahead/behind is shown as a signed delta.
