# Context Map

Each game is its own context with its own vocabulary.

## Contexts

- [Ninja Adventure](./ninja/CONTEXT.md) — third-person obstacle-course runner
- [Circle the Wagons](./wagons/CONTEXT.md) — first-person realistic stand in the middle of a stagecoach ring, shooting circling Riders
- [Grumpy Grandpa](./grandpa/CONTEXT.md) — 2D room-management game: fix or hide what napping Grandpa would hate, stage what he'd love, before he wakes
- [Nitro Racing](./nitro/CONTEXT.md) — third-person 3D arcade time-trial racer: six themed tracks, five cars with one ability each, coins unlock cars, beat your best time
