# Pirates of the Swingset

Glossary for Pirates of the Swingset, designed by Genevieve: a player swings
on a swingset while a pirate ship in the water ahead fires cannonballs at the
swings. The four Swingsets stand on themed Islands ringing the Ship's water,
connected by Zip Lines.

## Language

**Run**:
One full play session, from character choice until the player loses. Losing
all Hearts is the only way a Run ends.
_Avoid_: game, session

**Round**:
One battle against a single Ship. Won when that Ship sinks: Hearts refill
(dead Trees stand back up) and the next Round brings a slightly bigger,
tougher Ship. Broken Swings stay Broken between Rounds.
_Avoid_: wave, level

**Ship**:
The pirate ship in the water that aims and fires cannonballs. Each Round's
Ship is slightly bigger than the previous one. It aims at where a Swing
rests — so swinging high is the way to Dodge — but from Round 3 some shots
lead the Swing to its forward apex.
_Avoid_: boat, enemy

**Heart**:
One unit of the player's health. The player has five. Each Heart is embodied
by a living Tree near the current Swingset: losing a Heart makes a Tree fall
down dead.
_Avoid_: lives, HP

**Playground**:
The whole world of the game: four Islands in a ring around the water where
the Ship sails, each carrying a Swingset and its Trees.
_Avoid_: world, map, level

**Island**:
One of the four small islands of the Playground. Each has its own colours —
Jungle, Autumn, Snow, Volcanic — so it can be told apart even as a silhouette
in the fog. Islands connect only by Zip Lines.

**Swingset**:
A frame holding four Swings. Each Island carries one Swingset, facing the
Ship's water; the player plays at one Swingset at a time and the camera
stays zoomed in on it.

**Swing**:
One seat on a Swingset. A Swing can be Broken by a cannonball hit.
_Avoid_: seat

**Broken**:
The state of a Swing after a cannonball hits it. A Broken Swing cannot be
ridden. When all four Swings at the current Swingset are Broken, the player
must find another Swingset.
_Avoid_: destroyed, dead

**Last Stand**:
Play after all four Swingsets are Broken: the player fights on foot, dodging
blasts and throwing Tools, until the Hearts run out. It is the one time a
Tool may be thrown from the ground — at half power, with no Swing behind it.

**Trek**:
The journey from a wrecked Swingset to another one — a Zip Line ride between
Islands. During a Trek the Ship repositions, firing only occasional slow
shots.

**Lookout**:
Climbing a Tree to zoom the view out, survey the ring of Islands, and grab a
Zip Line. From the ground the neighbour Islands are only faint silhouettes in
the fog; the fog opens at a Lookout. Tool pickups sometimes wait at the top.

**Zip Line**:
A cable from a Lookout treetop down to a landing post on either neighbouring
Island. Press left or right at the treetop to ride toward that side. The ride
is locked on — it always delivers — but the Ship keeps firing, and a mid-ride
hit costs a Heart (a Tree falls on the destination Island). The camera plays
the ride as a short cinematic cut sequence. Zipping is allowed any time, not
just after a wreck — abandoning a live Swingset for fresh Trees is a choice.

**Pump**:
The one-button swinging input. It is a rhythm: a press lands PERFECT at
either end of the arc (where the Swing pauses), "good" a little off it, and
does nothing far off the beat. Only one press per half-swing counts, so
mashing climbs slowly and tops out low; keeping the beat reaches full height
in about six pumps. The Pump Ring over the rider shows the beat.

**PERFECT**:
A Pump pressed on the beat — the big push, with points. The Pump Ring
flashes gold.

**Pump Ring**:
The ring floating over the rider while swinging: its fill sweeps to the top
each half-swing; press when the tip is in the gold zone. Its core glows when
a throw right now would be a SUPER Throw.

**Dodge**:
A cannonball that flies under a rider who is up high on the arc. Shouted as
"DODGED!", worth points, and it banks a SUPER Throw for the next throw
(dodge → counterattack).

**SUPER Throw**:
A throw released near the top of a big forward swing — or the throw after a
Dodge: double damage, never misses, glowing trail, "SUPER!" pop. A SUPER
Wrench jams longer.

**Taunt**:
Pressing THROW on the ground: the kid faces the Ship and jeers (speech
bubble). The pirates get mad — their next shots come straight at the kid on
the ground, faster with every taunt — which drags fire away from the Swings.
Anger cools on its own. The first Taunt of a Run also hints that throwing
happens from a Swing.

**Bail**:
Jumping off a Swing to run along the ground — usually to escape an incoming
cannonball and reach a different Swing.
_Avoid_: dismount, eject

**Blast Radius**:
The area around a cannonball's ground impact. Being inside it when the ball
lands costs a Heart, so the ground is never safe.

**Tool**:
An item the player uses to fight back and damage the Ship. Tools are found
as pickups scattered around the Playground — sometimes up in a Tree. Tools
are thrown (and the Magnet catches) only from a Swing — the ground is for
fetching them, the Chainsaw, and Taunting; see Last Stand for the exception.
_Avoid_: weapon, item

**Boomerang Hammer**:
A Tool: a hammer thrown at the Ship that flies back to the player.

**Chainsaw**:
A Tool: cuts down a Tree, which the player then throws at the Ship.

**Giant Magnet**:
A Tool: catches a cannonball in mid-air, letting the player sling it back at
the Ship.

**Wrench**:
A Tool: thrown into the Ship's cannon to jam it, stopping its fire for a few
seconds.

**Tree**:
A tree near the Swingset, one per remaining Heart — the world's living health
meter. A standing Tree can be climbed as a Lookout. A Tree felled by the
Chainsaw or by a lost Heart becomes a throwable heavy projectile. A
chainsawed stump regrows quickly and costs no Heart; a Heart-lost Tree stays
dead until that Heart comes back.
_Avoid_: log (for a felled Tree)

**Character**:
The playable kid on the Swingset — the boy or the girl, unnamed. Chosen
before each Round; the player may switch between Rounds.

**Score**:
Points earned across a Run from many actions: hitting the Ship with Tools,
sinking Ships, time spent swinging (more the higher you swing), PERFECT
Pumps, Dodges, Swingsets found, Trees climbed. The best Score is saved in
the browser.
_Avoid_: high score (use "best")
