# Circle the Wagons

Glossary for Circle the Wagons, designed by Jeff: the player stands fixed in
the middle of a Ring of stagecoaches on a golden-hour prairie, turning freely
through 360°, and shoots the Riders who circle the Ring — endlessly — before
they shoot back.

## Language

**Run**:
One full play session, from the title screen until the player's Hearts run
out. Losing all Hearts is the only way a Run ends; there is no winning.
_Avoid_: game, session, level

**Raid**:
One named wave of Riders ("Raid 3"). A Raid ends when its last Rider is down;
Hearts refill, a short breather follows, and the next Raid is bigger, faster
and more aggressive.
_Avoid_: wave, round, level

**Ring**:
The circle of about eight Stagecoaches around the player, roughly twenty
yards across, with wide gaps between coaches. The player never leaves its
centre.
_Avoid_: circle, camp, wagon circle

**Stagecoach**:
One wagon of the Ring. Coaches are cover for Riders — a Rider passing behind
one is briefly hidden — and the player shoots through the gaps and over the
roofs.
_Avoid_: wagon, cart

**Rider**:
An outlaw on horseback circling outside the Ring. Riders are the only
targets. A Rider mostly circles one way at his own speed and radius; some
reverse, and later ones hang off the far side of the horse to be hard to hit.
_Avoid_: enemy, bandit, Indian, outlaw (as the noun for the target)

**Horse**:
The mount a Rider sits. A shot that hits a Horse brings it down — it skids
into the dirt, spilling its Rider (a Fall), then gets back up and gallops
off unharmed. A Horse whose Rider has Fallen gallops off out of the Ring.

**Fall**:
What happens to a Rider who is shot: he tumbles off his Horse as a physics
ragdoll (every Fall different), his hat flies off, and the body fades out a
few seconds later. No blood.
_Avoid_: death, kill, ragdoll (as the name for the event)

**Aim**:
A Rider's telegraph before shooting the player: he turns in the saddle and
raises his gun for about a second, with a visible and audible cue. Dropping
him during his Aim cancels the shot.
_Avoid_: telegraph, wind-up

**Heart**:
One unit of the player's health; the player has five. A Rider's shot always
costs a Heart (the player cannot move, so cannot dodge). Hearts refill at the
end of each Raid.
_Avoid_: lives, HP, health

**Rifle**:
The player's lever-action long gun: one accurate, powerful shot, then a lever
cycle before the next.

**Six-shooter**:
The player's revolver: six fast shots, then a spin-reload. The player toggles
between Rifle and Six-shooter at will; both have infinite ammo — reload time
is the only cost.
_Avoid_: pistol, revolver, handgun

**Reload**:
The pause after a gun empties (Rifle: after every shot; Six-shooter: after
six) during which the player cannot fire.

**Streak**:
Consecutive Riders Fallen without a missed shot. Shown on the HUD and
multiplies score.
_Avoid_: combo, chain

**Score / Best**:
One point (times the Streak multiplier) per Fallen Rider. Best is the highest
Score of any Run on this device.
_Avoid_: high score, kills
