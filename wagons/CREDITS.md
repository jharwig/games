# Credits — Circle the Wagons

Designed by Jeff.

Every third-party asset below is either CC0 (public domain) or CC-BY 4.0.
The CC-BY ones **require attribution**, which is what this file — and the
in-game credits screen fed by `src/credits.ts` — provide. Keep the two lists
in step whenever an asset is added or swapped.

Originals are downloaded by hand into the git-ignored `raw/` directory
(`tools/download-wizard.sh` walks you through it) and converted into
`public/media/` by `tools/process.sh`.
